First Principles · the carbon ledger

Before features, bedrock. A subscription is five primitives only. Everything else is packaging. The money itself is a state machine that does not care whether the customer is on a feature phone in Secteur 15 or a Flutter app.

Gateway-agnostic ledger events

intent→ authorized→ collected→ settled / failed / refunded / written-off

Checkout is a channel-agnostic state machine — web, USSD, or SMS wizard

identify→ consent→ method→ confirm

Rails & technical bedrock

  • Recurring is a standing customer authorization plus a scheduled obligation — pulls barely exist on BF MoMo.
  • The primitive is reminder-then-pay: invoice → SMS/USSD → confirm → wallet debit → merchant credit.
  • Each MNO is a settlement adapter behind one collect-once interface. Orange, Moov, Wave interchangeable.
  • Leave a vacant DirectDebitPort so PI-SPI can later upgrade reminder-pay to A2A debit without changing merchant contracts.
  • Separate the plan (what is owed) from the collection method (cash, MoMo, future bank debit).
  • Currency is a tenant/country attribute, not a hardcoded XOF constant, even while launching BF-only.
  • Phone number — MSISDN plus country code — is the customer primary key, not email.
  • Merchant identity is a registered business plus one payout destination, not a website.
  • Idempotent collection tokens: retries, double-SMS, webhook replays cannot double-charge.
  • One event bus. API merchants get webhooks; portal merchants get the same events in-app and SMS.

Merchant operational jobs

  • Bedrock job: stop chasing weekly or monthly money without hiring a collector.
  • Ship a living receivables book — who owes what, by when, who is late — not a generic subscription dashboard.
  • Proof-of-payment the merchant can show when a customer claims they already paid.
  • Register a client in under 60 seconds with only a name and a phone number.
  • Pause, skip, or prorate a cycle when service was not delivered — missed clean, gym closed, school holiday.
  • Make payout timing explicit: customer paid versus merchant received, with a visible settlement clock.
  • Grace periods and late fees as configurable policy, not custom code per merchant.
  • Failed-collection inbox: retry, switch rail, or mark cash without leaving the list.
  • Informal merchants start without a tax ID, with a later formalize path.
  • Clone a plan — weekly cleaning, 5,000 XOF — across many clients without retyping terms.
  • Bilingual customer messages (French plus Mooré/Dioula) that merchants do not compose themselves.
  • Employee/collector role: mark collected without seeing settings or payout details.

Customer experiential jobs

  • Bedrock job: stay in good standing with a trusted provider without walking to a kiosk every cycle.
  • One SMS screen must answer: what am I paying, to whom, how often, how much, by when.
  • Pick Orange versus Moov versus Wave based on which wallet has money today — not a locked-in rail.
  • A forwardable receipt the customer can send to a spouse or keep as proof.
  • A “not this week” pause that does not brand them a permanent defaulter.
  • Switch payment method mid-subscription without cancelling and re-enrolling.
  • Cancel from the phone without visiting the shop, with instant merchant notification.
  • Shared-phone households: authorize a payer who is not the service beneficiary.
  • Family or group pay: one wallet covers several children or compound members.
  • French-first copy with optional local language; never English-default amounts or dollar signs.
  • Feature-phone USSD path that completes the same consent as the hosted web page.
  • Show the next debit or due date before it happens so future auto-debit is never a surprise.

Job to Be Done · the two-sided loop

Stop chasing money, and stay in good standing, are the same loop seen from two stalls. Hire us for the shame-free receivables book plus the reminder-then-pay action — not for a dashboard that looks like San Francisco.

Merchant hires us to

stop chasing weekly or monthly money without hiring a collector.

  • Collect today for the clients who always forget
  • Automatic invoice SMS — stop typing reminders at 06:00
  • Agent / collector mode: a nephew visits and marks cash
  • Proof-for-landlord exports: water, compound security

Customer hires us to

stay in good standing without walking to a kiosk every cycle.

  • Pay with the wallet already in the pocket
  • Share the pay-link with whoever holds the money this month
  • Pause for travel, Ramadan, or school holiday without calling the shop
  • An SMS that says exactly what to type, or which USSD to dial

Online / API merchants

Look like Stripe in a week, not a quarter.

Hosted checkout keeps wallet secrets and PIN flows off the merchant’s servers. Webhooks unlock premium the moment collection clears. Plan-catalog API: monthly, annual, usage-like plans from their own UI. A customer billing-portal link so they never build “update my payment method.” Sandbox plus simulated wallets — QA never uses live Orange Money. Dunning API: failed payments retry without the merchant writing cron jobs. iOS, Android, and web share one merchant account. Localized checkout — French, XOF, MoMo brand marks — so conversion does not die on a card-shaped form. MRR, churn, failed-collection analytics a founder can show a partner.

Offline / portal merchants

Subscribe a cleaning client without downloading any app.

SMS onboarding for cleaning and tutor clients. Portal that works from a cybercafé PC or a mid-range Android browser. Bulk import from a notebook photo or an Excel of names and phones. Two-way SMS / WhatsApp: client replies OUI to pay. Printed shop QR that opens the customer’s current bill. Plan-template marketplace: weekly maid, school-fees term, mosque due, gym month. Later split-payout when there is a partner or franchise cut. “I am not a tech person”: photo of ID, talk-to-agent, we configure the plan.

Online customers

Finish in under two minutes on mid-range Android over 3G.

Create no new account — use the wallet already in use. Save this method: next month is one tap or one USSD confirm. A consumer home that lists every subscription across merchants on this platform. Grace: dispute or delay and still keep access for N days. Spoken or written amount confirmation before any wallet PIN is entered.

Offline and SMS-first customers

A short code saved in contacts as Loyer or Menage.

Voice IVR when reading a portal is too much. A neighbourhood agent who can take cash and mark me paid. Family-head view: pay three sisters’ salon or school plans from one phone. Reminder the day before due — load the wallet, not the morning of. Send-me-my-balance on demand via SMS keyword. An opt-out that actually works — regulatory and trust. Confirmation SMS whose merchant name matches the shop I already know. WhatsApp photo-receipt for people who will never open the web portal.

Platform, regulator, expansion

Jobs the courtyard also hired, quieter, at the back wall.

A country pack: new MNO, currency, language, KYC rules, without rewriting billing. PI-SPI / BCEAO readiness: be the first recurring layer when the public API opens. Regulator-friendly audit trail for a future e-money or payment-institution license. Merchant trust via take-rate-after-success or transparent settlement — not opaque holds. Ouaga and Abidjan developers: docs, Postman, Laravel and Flutter samples. Later org-payers: school canteen, mutuelle, church tithe — institutions, not just SMEs. Associations and guilds — coiffeuses, gyms, private schools — as the distribution job, not Facebook ads. A bank or MNO as silent partner: they want SME recurring volume; we want rails.

SCAMPER · seven dye vats

Afternoon in the market of substitutions. Each shutter is a lens. Open one. The stall behind it is not a feature list — it is a way of remaking checkout, receipts, risk, and the very idea of who initiates the bill.

Substitute — checkout, receipts, identity

Card PANs, email receipts, merchant-built UI, passwords, and a pooled payout wallet are the wrong objects for this street.

Card PAN forms become a wallet picker plus USSD / PIN handoff.
Email receipts become SMS plus WhatsApp, and an optional PDF for the few who want it.
Merchant-built UI becomes hosted checkout — redirect or iframe — and an optional white-label domain.
Monthly invoice becomes a prepaid merchant-wallet that auto-refills at a threshold.
Customer portal passwords become a magic SMS link that expires.
Our pooled payout wallet becomes the merchant’s existing Orange Business or Wave merchant code.

Combine — billing, channels, risk

One SMS should not be a reminder. It should be a complete journey. Channels split by job, not by fashion.

Invoice + payment link + USSD code in a single SMS.
Loyalty stamp-card with a recurring plan: 10th clean free, still monthly.
API merchant and portal merchant in one account: start on portal, add the API later.
Billing calendar combined with national holidays and end-of-month salary cycles.
Checkout combined with KYC-lite only when amount or risk crosses a threshold.
SMS for action, WhatsApp for receipt, email for the developer.

Adapt — regional and cultural patterns

Steal mental models that already live in West African hands: paybill, airtime stars, tontine, term fees, super-agents.

Adapt Stripe Checkout Session: createSession → hosted URL → webhook. Identical mental model.
Adapt M-Pesa paybill / till-number as a BF merchant “receive number.”
Adapt airtime short codes: *XXX*plan# to subscribe or pay.
Adapt tontine / e-susu rotating savings as a first-class recurring product type.
Adapt school-fees installment culture into term-peaked scheduled plans.
Adapt Orange / Moov super-agent networks as cash-in ramps when wallet balance is short.

Modify / Magnify — dunning, trust, developer hands

A single reminder is a whisper. A cadence is a street. Trust is a cheap thermal badge that says PAID.

Magnify reminders into a cadence: J-3 SMS, J-1 WhatsApp, J0 USSD push, J+2 merchant call list.
Modify amounts with family tiers, sliding scale, and rainy-season pause discounts.
Magnify trust with a cheap Bluetooth / thermal “PAID” badge the merchant can print in-shop.
Modify checkout into two steps: consent now, first collection on the due date — no immediate charge.
Magnify developer UX: one cURL, one webhook, copy-paste Laravel and Flutter samples.
Modify the portal for shared devices: staff PIN, short session, no wallet secrets on screen.

Put to other uses

The same checkout, the same SMS, the same ledger — sent into rent, DSTV-like bouquets, B2B credit, the shop floor.

Put the same checkout on one-off invoices so merchants start with “send a bill” before subscriptions.
Put SMS onboarding onto rent, water, guards, mosque, gym, private school, DSTV-like bouquets.
Put the ledger onto merchant-to-merchant supplier credit — B2B recurring.
Put the hosted page in-person: merchant SMS-es the link while the customer is in the shop.
Put gateway abstraction onto payouts later — salary-like recurring out.
Put the client directory to work as a mini-CRM: last service, notes, birthday, preferred rail.

Eliminate

What we refuse to ship is also a product decision. English. Card-network logos. Dollar signs. Passwords. Native apps for offline merchants.

Eliminate customer passwords — phone OTP or magic link only.
Eliminate native apps for offline merchants — mobile web plus PWA only.
Eliminate mandatory pay-now at subscribe; enroll first, debit on the agreed date.
Eliminate English, card-network logos, and $ defaults from every first-run screen.
Eliminate multi-page wizards: one screen for the plan, one screen to pay.
Eliminate platform-held customer balances if regulation allows pass-through settlement.

Reverse

Silence means success. The customer pulls the bill. The merchant is free until the first successful collection. BF is just the first country pack.

Reverse initiation: customer pulls the bill they owe — self-serve pay — instead of only being chased.
Reverse pricing: merchants free until first successful collection; take-rate after.
Reverse onboarding: customer invites a merchant (“I want to pay you monthly”) as demand-side growth.
Reverse notifications: silence means success; ping only on failure or change.
Reverse country expansion: ship adapter interfaces first; BF is just the first country pack.
Reverse dunning: reward early pay with airtime or an extra visit, not only punish late.
Reverse hosted checkout: stay on the merchant site via a pay-sheet web component.
Reverse fraud defaults: known payers are frictionless; new phones need extra OTP plus merchant confirm.

Constraint Inversion · weight as engine

An invented sitting: treat each hard constraint as a generative frame, not a limiter. Tap a laterite block. It turns over. Because literacy is low, because SMS is the OS, because PI-SPI is not out — therefore the product.

Because expansion is later — and other weights that still generate

Isolate strings, rails, KYC, and holiday calendars behind CountryPack. MSISDN parsing and wallet prefixes are data, never if-Burkina branches. Leave tax / e-invoicing hooks empty but present (DGI now, others later). Design a Côte d’Ivoire country pack on paper as the shadow second market. Pricing, contracts, and legal entity are per-country. One merchant group can run BF plus future CI with separate payout wallets. PI-SPI scheme readiness is the expansion strategy, not a BF-only feature. Prefer local MoMo aggregators over direct-integrating every new country rail. Keep a compliance calendar: BF license versus UEMOA passporting questions. All IDs are country-prefixed so tenants never collide when markets merge. A boutique or school can share one tablet kiosk as the checkout terminal. Treat *shortcode# as equal to https checkout, not a degraded mode. A recorded voice confirmation — “you will pay 5,000 every Monday to Awa Cleaning” — is a feature. Checkout and collector mark-paid must queue offline and reconcile. Onboarding must complete in one sitting under 15 minutes. Women run many informal services: design for shared phones, privacy, and a trusted female support line. Youth developers exist in Ouaga — the API channel is also a distribution channel. BCEAO moves slowly: the winner already has consented recurring mandates when the switch flips.

Dusk · the courtyard closes

Build the receivables-and-consent OS for Burkina Faso. Render it as Stripe-like checkout and as SMS / USSD. Collect via reminder-pay on Orange first. Leave the sockets open for every other rail, language, country, and future PI-SPI debit.

API versus portal is one product with two skins over the same checkout state machine and event bus. Splitting them into two systems would duplicate the real work.
Merchant job “stop chasing money” and customer job “stay in good standing” are the same loop: a shame-free receivables book plus a reminder-then-pay action.
No PI-SPI API is the wedge, not a hole. Reminder-pay is the honest BF primitive and stockpiles consented mandates that become the PI-SPI moat.
Hosted web, USSD, SMS verbs, and IVR are renderers of one consent protocol. Literacy constraints force that unification.
Cash as a first-class method is how informal merchants enter. Graduating cash payers to MoMo is the growth loop, not a v2 apology.
CountryPack plus rail adapters honor “multi-country from day one” without building Côte d’Ivoire. BF is the first pack, not the schema.
Stripe-like DX wins app merchants; 15-minute portal wins offline. One account that starts portal and later adds the API is the bridge.
Trust artifacts — named SMS, matching shop name, receipts, guilds, payout clock — will out-convert extra features in a cash culture.
Settlement finality (customer paid versus merchant received) is the hidden make-or-break. A pretty checkout that leaves money in limbo fails the bedrock job.
One-off “send a bill” is a quieter wedge than “start a subscription” for merchants who have never billed digitally.
Customer-invites-merchant and a cross-merchant consumer home are the same later bet: demand-side growth only after people already pay through us.
