- (decision) AD-2 Dependency rule: packages/domain has entities+ports+domain services only; never imports Nest/Next/pg/redis/rail SDKs. Adapters implement ports. apps/api composes adapters. Prevents domain leaking rail/HTTP types.
- (decision) AD-3 Ports catalog locked: PaymentRailPort.collect, DirectDebitPort (vacant NotImplemented), SmsPort, WebhookDeliveryPort, OtpPort, ClockPort/CalendarPort (CountryPack holidays). Prevents ad-hoc vendor calls from domain or portal.
- (decision) AD-4 Single Consent State Machine identify→consent→method→confirm for portal invite and Hosted Checkout Session. Same StandingAuthorization type. Prevents dual machines or password Customer accounts.
- (decision) AD-5 Reminder-then-pay only live debit: scheduled jobs may SMS, never PaymentRailPort.collect without a Customer confirm on that CollectionAttempt (or Cash Mark-paid by Merchant). DirectDebitPort must not be called in MVP. Prevents auto-debit and silent Orange pulls.

- (decision) AD-7 CountryPack is the only country switch: locale strings, rails, KYC rules, holidays, MSISDN parse, wallet prefixes, currency, timezone. Production pack BF only. No if-Burkina / hardcoded XOF / hardcoded +226 in product logic.
- (decision) AD-8 Tenancy: merchant_id on every tenant row; country_code from Merchant.CountryPack. Public IDs are ULID; country lives as attribute not prefix. Prevents UUID-vs-ULID drift and missing tenant filters.
- (decision) AD-9 Ledger: gateway-agnostic events intent|authorized|collected|settled|failed|written-off|reversed (refunded reserved, no Merchant-initiated refund in MVP). No Customer wallet balances. Pass-through to Merchant Payout Destination (Orange MSISDN). Do not implement platform float until counsel writes the control.
- (decision) AD-10 CollectionAttempt carries idempotency token; at-most-one live debit per token. Retry of same Obligation mints a new Attempt+token. Confirm, SMS double-tap, and webhook replay share this token.
- (decision) AD-11 Domain events persist via Postgres outbox in the same TX as the aggregate write; BullMQ webhook dispatcher is at-least-once; HMAC-SHA256 signature. Schedule 1m/5m/15m/1h/6h/24h then dead-letter. Merchant can replay from Developers. Prevents silent drop and unsigned callbacks.
- (decision) AD-12 Obligation birth only after StandingAuthorization confirmed (FR-53). Invite SMS and abandoned Session mint none. Due-or-past due at confirm = immediate Obligation. Reminder budget: one SMS Invoice + at most one reminder 48h later unless Merchant retries.
- (decision) AD-13 TakeRatePolicy: 0% on first successful live MoMo collect per Merchant; then 2.5% of Rail-collected integer XOF (half-up) at collected not settled. 0% fail/cash/Sandbox. Rail Reversal claws that Attempt fee; does not reset first-success. Policy object — not a scattered 2.5 constant in UI.
- (decision) AD-14 KycGate: block further payouts (not invoicing) when live MoMo collected XOF + Cash Mark-paid XOF exceed 500000 in a calendar month (CountryPack TZ) or Payout Destination changes while informal. Manual review acceptable in MVP.
