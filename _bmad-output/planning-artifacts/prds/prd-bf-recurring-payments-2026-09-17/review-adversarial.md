# Adversarial review

**Artifact:** `prd.md` + `addendum.md` (founder launch PRD, 2026-09-17).  
**Lens:** Adversarial (scope-leak / compliance / money-math). Hunt list applied in full.

## Verdict

**Not launch-ready as a closed source of truth.** Live-path locks (reminder-then-pay, no Customer wallet product, no live PI-SPI debit, no live USSD shortcode, no second-country pack, no card chrome) are written down. They are undermined by missing Subscription cancel, STOP that does not stop the commercial relationship, live consent copy that pre-announces future debit, and ledger/fee states (refund, write-off, partial collect) with no testable policy. UX and architecture must not treat FR-11, FR-31, FR-34, or FR-40 as closed.

## Findings

- **[critical]** Live consent markets future debit (FR-11) — Required checkout/SMS text says the Customer must confirm each collection “until a future debit capability is enabled,” and Standing Authorization is written so a later Direct Debit Port can reuse “the same consent meaning.” That is auto-debit marketing and bundled future-mandate consent in the live path, contradicting §5, FR-20, and §15 copy law. Addendum §1 still quotes system-idea “automated debits from Customer accounts.” *Fix:* MVP consent states reminder-then-pay only, forever-until-reconsent. Strike “until … enabled.” Require a new Standing Authorization version before any Direct Debit Port goes live. Move “automated debits” out of addendum §1 into LATER.

- **[critical]** Subscription cancel is promised and missing (FR-11, FR-31, UJ-2, §3) — Glossary defines Pause and Skip, not Cancel. Verbs are PAY / PAUSE / SOLDE / AIDE / STOP — no ANNULER. No FR for Customer or Merchant ending a Subscription. FR-11 still requires consent to say they can “PAUSE or cancel.” FR-31 and UJ-2 stop billing SMS only after “Subscription cancelled” or Client Record removal; removal itself has no FR (open Obligations, Standing Authorization, receipts, Webhooks undefined). Webhook `cancelled` is a Session status, not a Subscription state. *Fix:* Add FR-Cancel: Customer (SMS ANNULER) and Merchant can end the Subscription; refuse new Obligations; resolve or leave-due open ones; revoke Standing Authorization; stop billing SMS; emit a stable status. Define Client Record removal as the Merchant-side equivalent. Do not ship FR-11 copy until that FR exists.

- **[high]** STOP vs transactional is a compliance trap (FR-31, FR-18, FR-23, §15, addendum §7) — STOP cancels only “promo / tip / marketing-like” SMS. MVP has no such messages, so STOP is a no-op while invoices, PAY reminders, failure, receipts, and invites keep sending. ARCEP SVA subscriber-initiative STOP and Instruction 001-01-2024-style withdraw will be read as “end this.” Shared-phone spouse texts STOP; Fatou still gets billed. AIDE / unknown-text auto-replies after STOP are unspecified. Reminder cadence after STOP is unbounded (fights SM-C1). *Fix:* Publish the exact post-STOP allowlist. Disclose in 8th-grade French that STOP ≠ cancel. Route a second STOP (or ANNULER) to FR-Cancel. Cap PAY reminders. Do not send invite or AIDE-equivalent after STOP unless the Customer originated the text.

- **[high]** Refund and write-off are named, not specified (FR-34, UJ-4, addendum §2) — Ledger “supports refunded / written-off” and UJ-4 says owed until written-off (operator, not Customer). Consequences never define actor, trigger (Merchant vs platform staff vs Orange reversal), effect on Obligation, SMS, Payout Clock, or Webhooks. No glossary entries. Portal IA has no surface. “Staff-only” vs “operator” vs Merchant is undefined — no ops persona. *Fix:* Either drop refunded/written-off from MVP FR-34 or add FR-Refund and FR-Write-off with actor, Obligation outcome, Customer notice, and settlement/fee rules. If Orange can reverse a `collected` Attempt, that path is in-scope whether you want it or not.

- **[high]** Take-rate silent on partial collect, refund, and base amount (FR-40–42, SM-9, §12) — Policy is 0% then 2.5% of “successful MoMo collection amount.” No rule for: Orange daily-limit / partial success (MNO limit display is NEXT, so production will hit this); fee on collected amount vs Obligation vs Obligation+late-fee (FR-6); clawback if the first-ever success is later refunded/reversed (does the next collect stay 2.5%?); Sandbox `collected` counting as first success (FR-50 vs FR-40); fee timing (`collected` vs `settled`); failed settlement after `collected`. SM-9 cannot pass. *Fix:* Lock: Take-rate = 2.5% of Rail-collected XOF on Attempt ≥2; 0% if collected XOF = 0; partial success leaves remainder open on a new Attempt; refund/reversal of Attempt N reverses that Attempt’s fee and does not reset “first success ever”; Sandbox never increments the counter; late fee is inside the collected amount if collected. State rounding (XOF integers).

- **[high]** KYC threshold uses a different “success” than Take-rate (FR-43, FR-40, §9) — Gate is “cumulative successful collections” / month. Take-rate “first success” is MoMo/Orange only, not cash. Cash Mark-paid is a successful collection in SM-3. Informal Merchants can clear 500,000 XOF cash-only and never trip the gate — or trip it on cash the product treats as off-platform. *Fix:* Define the FR-43 sum as MoMo `collected` + Cash Mark-paid (or MoMo-only) in one sentence. Align SM-3 / FR-40 / FR-43 vocabulary.

- **[high]** Family-pay is the default happy path (UJ-2, FR-12, FR-17, §5) — Family/group pay is a non-goal, but Fatou shows the SMS to her husband, MSISDN is identity, and Orange confirm is a wallet PIN that need not be that MSISDN. Household payer ≠ enrolled beneficiary is normal BF MoMo and is exactly “family-pay.” Receipt, SOLDE, and Standing Authorization still attach to Fatou’s number. Theme map “Identity & household” invites the epic. *Fix:* MVP rule: enrolled Customer MSISDN is the consenting party; Rail wallet MSISDN may differ; do not add share-pay-link, second payer, or beneficiary fields. Disclose “whoever confirms on Orange pays for this number.” Keep household off the theme-to-FR path.

- **[high]** Customer-wallet / license language still ships (FR-17, FR-38, FR-45, UJ-3, addendum §2, §8.1) — Product forbids Customer wallets, then talks “wallet PIN,” CountryPack “wallet prefixes,” and “wallet debit.” Temporary platform float remains an open legal item; FR-38 only says not to expose it as a Customer wallet — holding funds can still be e-money issuance (§14–15). *Fix:* Say “Orange Money PIN / MSISDN prefix / Rail debit.” Add a hard non-goal: no platform-visible Customer balance, prepaid envelope, or merchant-wallet reload in MVP. Do not implement float until counsel writes the control; if float exists, it is operator-only and must not satisfy SOLDE.

- **[medium]** PI-SPI / Direct Debit Port can be built “because the socket must not be designed out” (FR-48, FR-11, §10, §15, addendum §1–2) — Live debit is Out of Scope, but FR-48 requires a vacant port, future-debit flag, and “ASAP” A2A readiness; §15 designs for Request-to-Pay. Easy to schedule a PI-SPI story under MVP architecture. *Fix:* MVP acceptance = no user control, no Adapter call, no sandbox toggle that posts a PI-SPI debit. RtP is LATER, not a hidden FR. Architecture may keep a named empty interface; product FRs stop at “do not call it.”

- **[medium]** Platform USSD can ship by confusing Rail USSD with the renderer (UJ-4, FR-49, NFR-8, addendum §2) — UJ-4: “Fatou cancelled the USSD confirm.” Addendum: Orange confirm is “typically USSD OTP.” FR-49 makes 1:1 USSD state mapping an MVP testable consequence; NFR-8 cites it as a quality bar. Implementers will add `*shortcode#` to “finish” FR-49. *Fix:* Rename the Orange step “Rail-wallet confirm (operator USSD/in-app — not our shortcode).” Drop 1:1 USSD mapping from MVP FR-49; keep “no live `*shortcode#`” only. NFR-8 tests SMS+web, not a future renderer.

- **[medium]** Second-country production can enter through docs and XOF (FR-45, NFR-9, addendum §4–5) — Production pack is BF-only; NEXT still has “Ouaga / Abidjan docs”; a “paper shadow pack” is allowed; XOF is also Côte d’Ivoire. A CI shadow plus shared currency plus Abidjan samples is a production toggle waiting for a flag. *Fix:* NEXT docs = Ouaga only. Paper CI pack is a dated appendix with no env flag, no MSISDN routing, no live keys. Assert Merchant CountryPack is BF for every MVP acceptance test.

- **[medium]** English / card chrome leaks on the Customer channel (FR-15, FR-30, NFR-1, SM-8, §21) — Hosted page bans English-default, `$`, and card marks. Customer SMS verbs are PAY, PAUSE, STOP (English) labeled “French verbs.” Glossary/IA terms (Magic Link, Sandbox, Webhook, Payout Clock, Take-rate, Stripe-like) will leak into SMS/help if not classified internal. Stripe-shaped docs invite card-checkout patterns. *Fix:* Customer verbs = PAYER / PAUSE / SOLDE / AIDE / STOP or ARRET (pick one, document matching). Ban PAY/PAUSE as the only documented forms. SM-8 includes SMS templates. Developer English IDs stay off Hosted Checkout Page and SMS. Stripe = Session→URL→Webhook job only; no card-network or `$` in sample screenshots.

- **[medium]** Glossary / synonym drift will fork epics (§3 vs running prose, addendum §1–2) — Forbidden or colliding terms still used: “user” (§2 Target User, NFR-1 “end users”); “client” vs Customer vs Client Record (UJ-1); “cancelled” = Session webhook vs Subscription end vs STOP; “portal” in addendum §1 (“access a portal”) vs Merchant Portal (consumer-home leak); addendum Subscription “five primitives” vs glossary Plan+Standing Authorization+Obligations; “collector” (JTBD) vs Collector mode OOS; “Payment status” (FR-17) vs Collection Attempt; “penalties” (addendum §1) vs late-fee; “recurring payments” (title/addendum) vs reminder-then-pay. *Fix:* Downstream uses glossary tokens only. Replace addendum §1 “portal” with Hosted Checkout Page. Add glossary Cancel, Refund, Write-off. One sentence: Webhook `cancelled` ≠ Subscription cancelled ≠ STOP.

- **[medium]** Several FRs are not testable as written (FR-2, FR-6, FR-11, FR-13, FR-22, FR-34, FR-48, FR-49, NFR-2) — FR-2/FR-7 durations are metrics, not acceptance tests (“typical 3G” undefined). FR-11/FR-48 “remains valid when Direct Debit Port attaches” cannot fail in MVP. FR-49 USSD 1:1 cannot fail without a renderer. FR-22 “attach a Rail without new account type” is design intent. FR-34 lists refunded/written-off with no consequences. FR-6 optional N-day interval is untestable. FR-13 “when space allows” is fuzzy. *Fix:* Move future-validity to NFR/architecture notes. Give every MVP FR a fail-in-staging consequence. Mark FR-2 as SM-1 only. Delete refunded/written-off from FR-34 or specify them.

- **[low]** Auto-debit / wallet wording in vision and collection primitive (§1, addendum §2) — Working title and addendum still say “recurring payments”; collection primitive says “wallet debit.” Internal readers will brief “auto-debit OS.” *Fix:* Title/subtitle stay receivables-and-consent / reminder-then-pay. Collection primitive: “Rail debit after Customer confirm.”

- **[low]** Issa / mosque / school color pulls vertical + family scope (addendum §8, §1 Vision) — Issa (school bursar), Imam/treasurer, “mosques,” loyalty example. Term frequencies are NEXT (FR-6) but personas invite term-billing and group treasurers into MVP stories. *Fix:* Keep color personas out of epic acceptance. MVP stories = Awa / Fatou / Adama only.
