# PRD Quality Review — BF Recurring Payments

## Overall verdict
This is a launch-grade source of product truth: a sharp thesis (a Burkina Faso receivables-and-consent OS, reminder-then-pay, one machine rendered twice), locked PO trade-offs with named give-ups, journeys that actually drive FRs, and success metrics that test the thesis rather than vanity activity. What is at risk for UX, architecture, and epics is the center of “recurring” — when Obligations are born, how often Fatou is pinged, how a feature-phone Customer confirms without a browser, and how a Subscription ends — all implied in journeys and STOP copy, never specified as FRs or listed as open. Those holes do not sink the document; they will be invented downstream unless they are written down. The §8 claim of zero phase-blockers overreaches that gap.

## Decision-readiness — adequate
The decisions that exist are stated as decisions, not “considerations.” Recurring is “**customer-authorized pay-on-reminder**, never a merchant-initiated wallet pull” (§1). Dual-channel is “rendered twice — not two products” (§1). Live Rail is Orange; Moov/Wave are designed-not-live; PI-SPI is sockets not debit; Take-rate is “2.5% … after the Merchant’s first successful MoMo collection ever” and “Not charged on failed Attempts or Cash Mark-paid” (Glossary, §12, FR-40–42). Informal KYC until “500,000 XOF” or payout-destination change (FR-43). STOP mapping is locked (FR-31). One Merchant account for portal + API (FR-3). Addendum §3 names what was given up (two products, auto-debit marketing, cards/English/`$`, CI production, native apps, Customer wallets, demand-side growth). Open Questions in §8 are actually open — owners and revisit conditions, not rhetorical setups.

A decision-maker pushing back on license, Orange access, or auto-debit expectation will find the objection in §8.1, the Risk table, Why Now, and Compliance. What keeps this from *strong* is the green-light sentence in §8: “Phase-blockers for UX/architecture/epics: **none**.” That is a decision about readiness, and it is smoother than the spec. The recurring clock, feature-phone confirm channel, and Subscription-ending actions are unasked questions, not locked PO answers. Fast path is allowed to infer; it is not allowed to declare the path closed while those inferences are missing. Cash-heavy mix versus SMS bundled into MoMo Take-rate is the other honest tension that is implied (SM-C4, FR-42) but not named as a give-up.

### Findings
- **medium** “Phase-blockers: none” overreaches (§8) — The locked PO set is real, but Obligation generation, reminder cadence, feature-phone confirm-without-browser, and Subscription cancel/write-off are unspecified and unlisted. A founder reading §8 would send this to UX/architecture/epics as closed. *Fix:* Keep the locked decisions; recast §8 as “no *known* blockers among decided items,” and promote the three high done-ness holes to owned open questions or `[ASSUMPTION]` lines with owners.
- **medium** Cash-only mix can burn SMS with zero Take-rate (§12, FR-42, SM-3, SM-C4) — FR-42: “SMS cost is absorbed in the MoMo Take-rate for MVP.” SM-3 treats Cash Mark-paid as a successful first cycle. SM-C4 says do not treat cash share as failure. Nobody states the give-up: Awa can hit the primary success metric while the platform pays SMS and collects 0%. *Fix:* `[NOTE FOR PM]` — accept subsidy until first Orange collect (and cap free SMS per Merchant), or accept that cash-only books are a GTM filter, not the happy path.

## Substance over theater — strong
Vision cannot be swapped into a generic payments PRD. “Offline Merchants chase clients on WhatsApp and in person. App teams rebuild a billing stack for every product. Existing aggregators sell one-shot pay-ins, not a living who-owes-what book” (§1) is market-specific and later backed by Why Now plus addendum §7 (CinetPay / PayDunya / LigdiCash / Flutterwave card-token “recurring”). Personas are not furniture: Awa, Fatou, and Adama each drive a FR cluster; Issa / Mariam / Imam are explicitly “not a lead UJ” / “color” in addendum §8. FR-2 even refuses slogan theater: “Time-to-first-invite is a measured success metric (SM-1), not an unmeasured slogan.”

NFRs are mostly product-specific (French-first, no `$` / card chrome, T+1 *target*, idempotent debit, Staff PIN hashing, STOP vs transactional). NFR-11’s “shame-free” is bounded by “No public defaulter wall, no shaming ranking.” CountryPack, Direct Debit Port, and USSD-capable model are future sockets with `[NON-GOAL]` / Out of Scope attached — disciplined constraints, not a second product in costume. FR-22’s testable consequences are the thinnest (“Rail is an enumerated CountryPack field”), but they do not pretend Moov/Wave are shipping.

## Strategic coherence — strong
The thesis is one sentence: there is no receivables-and-consent OS for informal BF Merchants and developer Merchants; aggregators sell one-shot pay-ins; live MoMo debit is payer-approved, so recurring means reminder-then-pay plus a ledger, not auto-debit. Features follow that arc — one Consent State Machine, portal + API on one account, Orange + Cash Mark-paid, SMS as OS, Payout Clock as the honesty product, informal KYC, take-rate after first MoMo success. MVP scope kind is problem-solving (who-owes-what + collect) with an experience cut (French SMS sitting, shame-free, cash as equal). Scope logic matches: templates, WhatsApp, live USSD, second-country pack, and consumer Super-App are cut because they steal focus from the five primitives (addendum §2, §4).

Success Metrics validate the thesis, not activity: SM-1/SM-2 measure the informal sitting; SM-3 the first collected cycle (including cash, which the thesis insists is first-class); SM-4 zero double-charge; SM-5 Adama’s week-to-sandbox; SM-6/SM-7 settlement honesty; SM-8 French-first integrity; SM-9 take-rate correctness. Counter-metrics SM-C1–C4 are load-bearing — especially SM-C1 (do not maximize SMS) and SM-C4 (do not treat cash share as failure). This is not a backlog with headings. Addendum §9 even forbids turning the theme map into FRs.

### Findings
- **low** SM-3 can go green with no live Orange (§7 SM-3) — “at least one `collected` Orange Attempt **or** Cash Mark-paid” means the collection primitive the Why Now section bets on can fail while the primary 30-day metric passes. Coherent with “cash is the on-ramp,” but it does not validate FR-21. *Fix:* Keep SM-3 as the Merchant-outcomes metric; add a launch gate (secondary SM or ops criterion) for first live Orange `collected` before calling the Rail path done.

## Done-ness clarity — adequate
Most FRs carry at least one testable consequence, and the document is unusually willing to name exact strings, statuses, and numbers: consent text must include Merchant name, amount rule, frequency, next due date, confirm-each-time, PAUSE/cancel (FR-11); no “prélèvement automatique” (FR-11); webhook statuses `authorized | collected | failed | expired | cancelled` (FR-16); Take-rate 0% then 2.5% (FR-40); KYC gate 500,000 XOF / calendar month (FR-43); STOP vs transactional mapping (FR-31). Soft adjectives (“user-friendly,” “reasonable performance”) are rare. NFR-2’s “< 8 seconds on a mid-3G profile” is tagged `[ASSUMPTION]`, which is the right honesty move.

The dimension is not *strong* because the FRs that exist specify skins and gates while the recurring engine is atmospheric. FR-6 records “reminder timing defaults” without a default. FR-20 allows “Scheduled jobs may send SMS Invoice / reminders” and only forbids debit. FR-27 proves a generator exists (“While Paused, no new Obligations are generated”) but never says when the nth Obligation is created, whether it can exist before Standing Authorization, or how many reminders fire. UJ-2’s Customer is “Feature phone or 3G Android” yet the climax confirm is “on the Hosted Checkout Page.” Cancel, write-off, refund, and Client Record removal appear as facts in journeys and FR-31/FR-34 without a done condition. Several consequences leave an unresolved OR (FR-7 “rejected or merged”; FR-14 “Plan (or equivalent line items)”). Downstream story creation will fill those blanks.

### Findings
- **high** Recurring Obligation generator and reminder cadence are implied, not specified (§4.2 FR-6, FR-9; §4.4 FR-20, FR-27; §4.5 FR-29) — FR-6: “Collection preferences record reminder timing defaults; they do not lock a Rail.” FR-20: “Scheduled jobs may send SMS Invoice / reminders; they must not debit.” No FR states: create Obligation on/before due date; send one SMS Invoice at T−N; send at most one reminder; do / do not generate Obligations while Standing Authorization is still pending (UJ-1 resolution allows “pending or confirmed”). SM-C1 cannot be enforced without a cap. *Fix:* Add one FR (schedule + reminder budget + consent-before-bill rule) with numeric defaults tagged `[ASSUMPTION]` if the PO did not lock them.
- **high** Feature-phone confirm still depends on a hosted page (UJ-2, FR-12, FR-21, NFR-2) — UJ-2 persona is “Feature phone or 3G Android. No email.” Path step 6: “Confirms the Collection Attempt on the Hosted Checkout Page.” NFR-2: “completable from SMS verbs plus a low-weight Hosted Checkout Page.” FR-21 allows “Hosted Checkout Page or PAY path.” A browser-less feature phone cannot complete hosted confirm; Orange collect in-market is typically USSD/in-app approve after a merchant-initiated push. UX will invent the PAY → OTP → Orange-USSD path — or silently drop feature phones. *Fix:* Lock a no-browser confirm path (SMS verb + OTP + Rail USSD) as in-MVP, or mark browserless feature phones `[NON-GOAL for MVP]` and correct UJ-2.
- **high** Subscription ending and write-off/refund have no FR (UJ-2, UJ-4, FR-31, FR-34) — UJ-2: “billing SMS continue until the Subscription is cancelled or Awa removes her.” FR-11 requires copy that they can “PAUSE or cancel.” FR-31: “Removing the Client Record or cancelling the Subscription stops billing SMS.” UJ-4: “write-off is operator, not a Customer self-serve in MVP.” FR-34 “supports refunded / written-off as ledger states (write-off / refund may be staff-only in MVP).” An engineer cannot know who may cancel, what happens to open Obligations, or whether refund is in MVP. *Fix:* Add FRs for Merchant cancel, Customer cancel, Client Record removal side effects, and operator write-off — or `[NON-GOAL for MVP]` refund and Customer-initiated cancel.
- **medium** Unresolved ORs and unbounded expiries in otherwise testable FRs (FR-7, FR-12, FR-14, FR-27) — FR-7: “rejected or merged with an explicit choice.” FR-14: “Plan (or equivalent line items that become a Plan/Obligation).” FR-12/FR-14: Magic Link, OTP, and Session “expire” with no TTL. FR-27: “Resume is available … via a documented SMS/path” — path unnamed. *Fix:* Lock Plan ID on Session create for MVP; pick reject *or* explicit merge; state TTLs as `[ASSUMPTION]`; name the resume verb.

## Scope honesty — strong
§5 Non-Goals and §6.2 Out of Scope do real work: not a PI/e-money issuer, not a Customer wallet, not auto-debit, not live PI-SPI, not live Moov/Wave, not second-country production, not native apps, not cards/English/`$`, not a named one-off product, not family/group pay, not live USSD, not demand-side growth, not tontine/credit/NGO. Inline `[NON-GOAL for MVP]` on FR-9 (named one-off invoicing) and FR-22 (live Moov or Wave) sit at the exact places a reader would silently promote a socket into a ship. Addendum §4–5 is a NEXT/LATER catalog with one-line reasons, and §6.2 warns “do not promote them into FRs.” Assumptions are tagged at the inference (FR-6 frequencies, FR-36 holidays, FR-38 payout MSISDN, FR-43 manual review, NFR-2 8s, §19 residency) and mostly indexed. Open Questions have owner + revisit. De-scope of CI, USSD, WhatsApp, templates is argued, not slipped.

The cancel/scheduler cluster is an underspecified *inclusion*, not a hidden omission of a rival product — that belongs in Done-ness, not here. Open-item density is appropriate for locked-PO fast path (six owned questions, one `[NOTE FOR PM]`, a short Assumptions Index). The overclaim is the “none” in §8 (Decision-readiness), not a missing Non-Goals section.

### Findings
None.

## Downstream usability — strong
This is chain-top (UX → architecture → epics); the dimension is load-bearing and the PRD is built for extract. Glossary §3 orders downstream to use terms “exactly” and calls synonym drift “a discipline violation.” Domain nouns (Merchant, Customer, Client Record, Standing Authorization, Obligation, Collection Attempt, Rail, Cash Mark-paid, Payout Clock, CountryPack, Take-rate, Reminder-then-pay) are stable across UJs, FRs, NFRs, and SMs. “Not ‘user’” is honored. UJ-1–UJ-5 each name a protagonist and carry context inline (Awa / Fatou / Adama). FR-1…FR-50 and NFR-1…NFR-12 and SM-1…SM-9 / SM-C1…SM-C4 are unique and complete; UJ capability lines resolve to real FR IDs. Sections can be pulled with glossary terms rather than “see above.” Addendum correctly disclaims itself: “not a second source of truth for in-scope FRs.”

FR-18 and FR-19 appear after FR-28 in document order (grouped under Notifications) — IDs are contiguous in number space, not in reading order. That is a mechanical quirk, not an extraction failure. “Receivables book” and “send-a-bill” are working prose without glossary entries; they will not confuse a story writer who already has Obligation + Collection Primitive.

### Findings
None.

## Shape fit — strong
The agreed form is Journey-led + Vision/Features hybrid for a founder launch PRD that feeds UX, architecture, and epics. That matches the product: multi-stakeholder (informal Merchant, Customer, Developer Merchant), meaningful UX (SMS + hosted French checkout + portal), not a single-operator internal tool. Five UJs with named protagonists are load-bearing, not overhead; capability FRs sit under vision-grouped features without collapsing the journeys into a backlog. Brownfield-style existing-code claims are absent (greenfield / system-idea). Regulatory traceability is present at the concern level (WAEMU/BCEAO, Instruction 001-01-2024, ARCEP SVA, PI-SPI) without turning the PRD into a compliance matrix. Hybrid form creates some repetition (monetization in §12 and FR-40–42; surfaces in §11 and UJs) — readable, not a shape mismatch. Extra personas stay in the addendum. Not over-formalized, not under-formalized.

### Findings
None.

## Mechanical notes
- **ID placement, not ID gaps.** FR-1–FR-17, FR-20–FR-28, then FR-18 and FR-19, then FR-29–FR-50. All IDs 1–50 exist; 18–19 are section-grouped under Notifications. No duplicates. UJ-1–5, SM-1–9, SM-C1–C4, NFR-1–12 are clean.
- **Assumptions Index roundtrip is incomplete.** Inline but not indexed: NFR-2 (“Hosted Checkout Page first contentful interaction targets < 8 seconds…”) and §19 residency (“MVP hosting may be outside BF if legal allows”). Indexed but not inline-tagged: system-idea “Customer logs in” = Magic Link/OTP; Take-rate “first successful collection ever” = MoMo/Orange not cash; late-fee compounding “refine in UX” (the last is also Open Question 4).
- **Glossary drift (light).** “Receivables book,” “send-a-bill,” “Developers surface/toggle,” and “MoMo” (vs Orange Money / Rail) are used as if they were glossary terms. “Payment status” in FR-17 vs Collection Attempt status elsewhere. None collide enough to break extract.
- **UJ protagonists.** UJ-1 Awa, UJ-2 Fatou, UJ-3 Adama, UJ-4 Awa, UJ-5 Awa — all named, context inline. No floating UJs.
- **Required sections for launch / chain-top.** Present: Vision, Target User + UJs, Glossary, Features/FRs, Non-Goals, MVP in/out, Success Metrics + counters, Open Questions, Assumptions Index, Why Now, surfaces, Monetization, NFRs, Constraints, Compliance, Ops, Integrations, API capability, Data Governance, Risk, Tone, IA. Technical-how and rejected alternatives correctly live in `addendum.md`.
